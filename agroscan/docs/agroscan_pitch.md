# AgroScan Pitch (Summary)

## Problem
Crop stress is often detected late; farmers need affordable aerial scouting to spot issues early.

## Solution
- Lightweight drone missions collecting RGB/NIR imagery
- NDVI-style vegetation indices computed on-device or near-edge
- Dashboards flag hotspots for targeted intervention

## Differentiators
- Cost-sensitive bill of materials
- Mission templates for smallholder parcels
- Clear data-to-advice pipeline

## Metrics (targets)
- <48h turnaround from flight to advice
- 15-20% reduction in water/fertilizer overuse on pilots
- Sub-meter georegistration on stitched orthomosaics
